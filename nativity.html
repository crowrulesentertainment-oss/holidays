THE CHRISTIAN STORY OF CHRISTMAS

The Nativity of
Jesus Christ.

For Christians around the world, Christmas Day is a celebration of the birth of Jesus Christ — a story of hope, humility, peace, and God's love.

THE BIBLICAL ACCOUNTS

A story of hope.

The New Testament Gospels of Matthew (chapters 1–2) and Luke (chapters 1–2) tell the biblical accounts associated with Jesus's birth. The accounts include Mary and Joseph, Bethlehem, and the beginnings of the life Christians understand as central to their faith.

Moments in the story

01

The Annunciation

In Luke's Gospel, the angel Gabriel tells Mary that she will bear Jesus.

02

Bethlehem

Luke describes Mary and Joseph traveling to Bethlehem, where Jesus is born and laid in a manger.

03

The Shepherds

Luke recounts an angelic announcement to shepherds, who go to see the child.

04

The Magi

Matthew tells of Magi who follow a star and bring gifts. The familiar details of Nativity scenes combine passages and later Christian traditions.

Ways Christians celebrate

✦
Advent

A season of preparation and expectation leading up to Christmas in many Christian traditions.

✦
Church services and prayer

Many attend Christmas Eve or Christmas Day worship, including Midnight Mass in some traditions.

✦
Carols and Nativity displays

Music, Scripture readings, plays, and Nativity scenes help communities retell the story.

✦
Giving and hospitality

Families and congregations may mark the day through generosity, meals, service, and welcoming others.

A note on dates and history

December 25 is the traditional Christmas date for many Christians, but the exact historical date of Jesus's birth is not known. Some Orthodox communities celebrate on January 7 in the Gregorian civil calendar because they follow a different calendar for fixed feasts; practices vary. This page presents Christian beliefs respectfully and distinguishes scripture from later customs and historical questions.
