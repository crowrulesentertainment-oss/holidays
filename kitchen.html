WARM & COZY
10 MIN·EASY

Classic Cozy Cocoa

Warm milk, cocoa, a little sweetness, and your favorite toppings make a comforting cup for a chilly evening.

You'll need

Milk or plant-based alternative · cocoa powder · sugar or sweetener · pinch of salt · optional vanilla

  1. Whisk cocoa, sweetener, and salt with a small splash of milk until smooth.
  2. Warm with the remaining milk over medium-low heat, stirring; do not let it boil over.
  3. Add vanilla if desired and serve warm with toppings of choice.
BAKE TOGETHER
VARIES·FAMILY ACTIVITY

Decorate-Your-Own Cookies

Turn a batch of plain cookies into a colorful tradition with icing, sprinkles, and a little imagination.

You'll need

Fully baked, cooled cookies · prepared icing · assorted decorations · plates or parchment

  1. Set out cooled cookies and small bowls of decorations.
  2. Let each person decorate a cookie with a theme or story in mind.
  3. Take a photo of everyone's creations and choose one to recreate next year.
GATHER ROUND
30+ MIN·FLEXIBLE

Make-It-Your-Own Soup Night

A flexible gathering idea: prepare a favorite soup and let everyone add toppings or share a family food memory.

Build your table

Favorite soup · bread or crackers · herbs · optional toppings clearly labeled for dietary needs

  1. Choose a soup recipe that fits the group's dietary needs.
  2. Prepare toppings in separate labeled bowls.
  3. Invite everyone to share a food memory or suggest a recipe for next time.
