Birthday Notifications

Manage a local reminder preference and learn how scheduled email notifications are activated.

Browser reminder preference

Browser notifications require your permission and only work while the site or its service worker is active. They are not scheduled emails.

Scheduled email delivery

Automatic emails require a server-side scheduled job, such as a Supabase Edge Function called by a scheduler, plus an email provider and verified sender domain.

  1. Deploy the included supabase/functions/birthday-reminders/index.ts.
  2. Set the service-role secret and email-provider secret as server-side function secrets only.
  3. Schedule the function daily and add opt-in records to the reminder preferences table.

This package includes the deployment starter and SQL table, but does not enable a scheduler or send messages until you configure them.
