SMALL MOMENTS. BIG MEMORIES.

A little inspiration
goes a long way.

Simple ways to bring people together, make something with your hands, and add a little sparkle to an ordinary day.

01 / CREATE
✂

Make a memory card

Decorate a card for someone who made your year brighter. Add one specific reason you're grateful for them.

15–30 MINUTES
02 / CONNECT
♡

Kindness quest

Choose three small acts of kindness: help with a task, call someone you miss, or leave an encouraging note.

ANYTIME
03 / CELEBRATE
✦

Build a tradition

Pick a favorite snack, song, story or walk and repeat it every year. The little rituals become the big memories.

MAKE IT YOURS
04 / PLAY
❄

Seasonal scavenger hunt

Make a list of five things that remind you of the current season and see how many your group can spot together.

FAMILY FRIENDLY
05 / REMEMBER
✉

Write a future note

Write down a hope for the next season. Seal it, set a reminder, and open it when that season returns.

A MOMENT TO REFLECT
06 / SHARE
☕

Host a cozy hour

Invite someone for a warm drink, a favorite playlist and an unhurried conversation. No elaborate plan required.
